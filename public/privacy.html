01 At a glance

This page describes the privacy approach for the Bandana portfolio website (“the site”). It is a personal demonstration project. It is not a client portal, account service, or production agency platform.

02 Information

The site does not ask you to create an account or intentionally collect information through its contact form. That form currently runs only in your browser: submitting it displays a confirmation, but does not transmit or persist your name, email address, or message.

Like most websites, the web host or infrastructure provider may process basic connection information—such as an IP address, request time, browser type, and pages requested—in server or security logs. Whether and how long those logs are kept depends on the provider and its configuration.

03 How information is used

Information handled by the hosting provider may be used to deliver the site, diagnose errors, protect the service, and prevent abuse. The site itself does not use contact-form entries for marketing, profiling, or automated decisions because those entries are not sent to it.

04 Cookies & analytics

The site does not currently include analytics or advertising tools, and it does not intentionally set cookies for tracking. Your browser or the hosting provider may still use essential technical mechanisms to deliver or secure the site; check your browser settings and the provider’s privacy information for details.

05 Third-party services

The site may be delivered through a hosting provider, which necessarily handles requests to serve its pages. This policy does not replace that provider’s own privacy terms. No payment processor, embedded social feed, or advertising network is intentionally part of this demo.

06 Retention & security

The site does not keep contact-form submissions. Any technical logs are controlled by the hosting provider and retained under its settings and policies. Reasonable care is taken in building and maintaining the site, but no website or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure.

07 Your choices

You can choose not to enter personal details into the demo form. If you have a privacy question or a request about information that may appear in hosting logs, contact the site owner using the contact details provided with this portfolio; requests concerning provider logs may need to be directed to the hosting provider.

08 Children

This portfolio is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect information from children. Please do not submit personal information through the demonstration form.

09 Changes & contact

This notice may be updated if the site’s features or hosting arrangements change. The date above indicates the latest revision. For questions about this notice, contact the site owner through the contact details provided with this portfolio.

This page is written for a personal portfolio demonstration and is not legal advice or a substitute for a policy prepared for a live commercial service.
